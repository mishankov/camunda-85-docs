Camunda 8 SaaS status

Camunda 8 SaaS is a hosted service for the Camunda 8 stack that runs on the Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Like any service, it might occasionally undergo availability changes. When availability changes, Camunda makes sure to provide you with a current service status.

To see current and past service availability, visit Camunda 8 SaaS Status.

Subscribe to updates

Don’t want to check the service status page manually? Get notified about changes to the service status automatically.

To receive service status updates:

  1. Go to the Camunda 8 SaaS Status page and click SUBSCRIBE TO UPDATES.
  2. Select Atom and RSS feeds.
  3. Add the feed URL to your favourite Atom/RSS reader.
  4. After you subscribe to updates, you are notified whenever a service status update is posted.

Support

Support can be requested by subscription or enterprise customers via JIRA. Otherwise, contact us. For more information about Enterprise support and additional support resources, see Enterprise Support.
